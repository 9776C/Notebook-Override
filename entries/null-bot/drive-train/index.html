    Brainstorm¶

    X-drive:¶

    4 wheels, at a 45 degree angle, in each corner of the bot

    Source

    Pros: - Highly maneuverable - Turns well - Power is consistent

    Cons: - Bad motor efficiency - Less overall power - Unconventional design, not as optimal - Lower power cap

    Mecanum:¶

    45-degree wheels on each wheel

    Source

    Pros: - Highly maneuverable - Much better efficiency - Higher power cap

    Cons: - Harder to design/code - Power is not as consistent

    H-drive:¶

    4-motor drive, with a perpendicular wheel to move bot horizontally without turning

    Source

    Pros: - Slightly more maneuverable than 4 motor drive

    Cons: - Not as maneuverable as other ideas - Least traction/friction
